## Order of work (v1, history)
1. **Region 1 (sewers), playable first:** ✅ all built: tiles, traps/plants/heaps, set pieces, items, skeleton, Warrior (7 tiers), sewer monsters, Ghost-quest mini-bosses, Goo, Ghost, Rat King (last few waiting for approval)

**Character rules (decided 2026-11-23):** characters are **voxels at the items' voxel size (1/32)**, but **designed as proper humanoids in T-pose** (real torso, arms, legs; `heroes/voxel_char.py` body plan), NOT a copy of the sprite's pose. The sprite only supplies the look: hair/face/beard design and each tier's colour roles. **A full model per armour tier** on the class's one skeleton: `hero_<Class>_t0..t6.glb`. Base look = cloth. Rejected: stacked boxes with stretched sprite pixels; smooth rounded models (off-theme); voxels copying the sprite's squashed pose.
2. **Remaining heroes:** Mage ✅ → Rogue ✅ → Huntress ✅ → Duelist ✅ → Cleric 🟡 (7 armour tiers each, shared `heroes/hero.py`)
3. One region at a time: prison, caves, city, halls (tile kit, monsters, boss, set pieces)
4. Vault and mining quest areas, remaining items, VFX

## Known open points
- Engine: walls between the camera and the hero hide the row behind them; needs a cut-away/fade in the engine.
- Plant cards look thin seen from straight above.
- Items over the 300-triangle budget: Ethereal Chains (418) and Whip (thin, wiggly sprites).
- Warrior stands 1.03 tall with his hair crest (spec says ~0.9 for a human); scale down if the engine needs it.
- Armour tiers differ by colour (+ shoulder pads from scale up); per-tier shapes (mail skirt, plate helmet...) can be added in `voxel_char.body`.
- Not yet seen in the real engine: model swap per tier and clip playback need a check there.
- Round voxel shapes (slime dome) show small stair-step self-shadows under a low, hard light; the engine's lighting decides how visible they are.
- Blended (smooth) joints keep faces 1x1 along the bend and drop hidden inner faces (`voxel_mesh`), so bends never crack open.
- Goo is near-black like the sprite; on a dark floor it relies on its gloss streak and the engine's lighting to read.
- A creature that scales in any clip keys scale in every clip (`"scale": {}`), or an engine may carry the scale over between clips.
- `rig.floor_lifts` keeps key frames above the floor; the rat, snake and earlier creatures were checked by hand instead and may dip ~1-2 cm between keys.
- `hero_roles` moves a body colour spot inward when a slimmer sprite has a transparent pixel there (the Huntress's legs); wider heroes are unaffected (checked).
- Voxel colour patterns should run in stripes, not checkers: a checker stops face merging and doubles the triangles.
- Colour picks use `opaque_rgb` (fails on transparent pixels, which read as white) in the hero scripts; older creature scripts still use `pixel_rgb` with asserts.
- Every creature build clears the Blender file's clips first (`make_armature`), so a file never ships another creature's clips.
- Monster sprites are mostly side views: their voxels will extrude across the body's width (sprite length -> forward), unlike heroes (front view).
- Item grips are placed by a rule (handle = bottom-left of the sprite). Shields, gloves and the bow will need a hand-placed grip when the hero holds them.
- Doors in north–south walls read less clearly than in east–west walls.
- `shortcut:` comments in the scripts mark accepted limits (list them with `/ponytail-debt`).
